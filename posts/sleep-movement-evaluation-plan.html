Sleep Health & Movement Evaluation Plan

September 07, 2026 Health

This plan covers my health issues related to sleep, particularly movement during sleep: periodic leg/body twitching and vocalization. This has a significant impact on Sheryl, my partner. The issue tends to correlate with stress and late-night sugar, but can go many nights without incident.

I am very averse to pharmaceutical medications, so my focus is on nutrition, exercise, physical alignment (Alexander Technique, Tai Chi), and Eastern / integrative diagnosis and treatment options.

Medical History

  • Overnight Wired Sleep Study: Minimal apnea observed, some periodic limb movement (PLM). No hard clinical recommendations at the time.
  • Acupuncture: 6-session series focusing on sleep quality and nervous system relaxation.

Standardized Clinical Self-Evaluations

To comprehensively track leg motion, sleep quality, and potential neurological contributors over time, regularly completing standardized self-assessments provides objective baseline data:

  • RLSRS (Restless Legs Syndrome Rating Scale): Assesses severity of RLS symptoms, frequency, intensity, and impact on sleep.
  • IRLSSG Rating Scale: Quantifies severity of RLS in research and clinical settings.
  • PSQI (Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index): Global measure of sleep quality and disturbances over the past month.
  • ESS (Epworth Sleepiness Scale): Measures general daytime sleepiness across routine situations.
  • BDI-II & GAD-7: Standardized screenings for mood and anxiety factors that correlate with neuromuscular twitching.
  • Sleep Diary & Actigraphy: Daily tracking of bedtime, wake time, sleep latency, awakenings, and dietary triggers.
