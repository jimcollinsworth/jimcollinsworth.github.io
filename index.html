Sleep Health & Movement Evaluation Plan

[SPEC] • Sep 07, 2026 • Mine

Movement during sleep is the problem: leg/body twitching and vocalization. A structured plan focusing on nutrition, exercise, somatic alignment (Alexander Technique, Tai Chi), and standardized self-evaluations.

Standardized baseline clinical assessments tracked over time include the Restless Legs Syndrome Rating Scale (RLSRS & IRLSSG), Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI), and Epworth Sleepiness Scale (ESS) alongside daily sleep diary notes.
