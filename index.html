Welcome. I'm Jim Collinsworth. After a 50-year career spanning business consulting at Arthur Andersen, professional guitar, software startups, and data science, I'm now exploring the next chapters of life and technology from Chicago.

"How does that work?" is the question that has guided my entire life — whether looking at software architecture, electrical wiring, human biomechanics, music theory, or the evening sky over Lake Michigan. This site is my open notebook for venturing outside narrow specializations and exploring across boundaries.

Active Lanes

Health & Movement

Tai Chi practice, Alexander Technique, sleep mechanics, periodic limb movement analysis, and non-pharmacological care.
