I'm newly retired this year after 50 years of working with software and clients. 20 years at Arthur Andersen, then as a guitarist, back to software and a couple startups, and finally finishing up with a data science job after the pandemic. Now I get to sit back (hopefully) and enjoy the family, continue learning, exercising, the Chicago lakefront, AI and the next few decades of amazing change and innovation.

Curiosity & Mindset

"How does that work?" is the question that is always in my mind, and comes up for everything: software, hardware, electricity, biology, society, law, music, art, psychology... I'll be the first to admit something I don't know, but at the same time will be fleshing out the alternatives, questioning everything, devising an educated guess and following up as soon as I can to understand.

Lanes & "Out of My Lane"

The name of this site reflects my habit of venturing into domains well outside my core training. Rather than siloing myself into a single specialty, I organize my active pursuits and projects into thematic Lanes:

Music & Art

Guitar, piano, music studies, drawing, and photography.

Health & Movement

Tai Chi, anatomy and somatic education (Alexander Technique), sleep health and motor tracking.

Software & Computing

Data analysis & visualization, site development & operations, home automation, AI, and local offline assistants.

Making & Projects

Woodworking, hand-carved tool handles, lutherie setup, knife making, and DIY fabrication.

References & Reading

Curated books, research papers, essential URLs, and key quotes.
