About Jim and His Lanes

Chicago, Illinois • Lifelong Learner & Maker

I am newly retired after 50 years of working with software, systems, and clients. My career took me through 20 years at Arthur Andersen, followed by several years as a working guitarist, a return to tech across several software startups, and finally capping off with a data science role in the years following the pandemic.

Now I have the privilege to sit back (or rather, dive in on my own terms), enjoy my family, continue learning, exercise, walk the Chicago lakefront, explore AI, and witness the next few decades of rapid innovation.

"How does that work?" is the question that is always in my mind. It comes up for everything: software, hardware, electricity, biology, society, law, music, art, psychology...

I'll be the first to admit when I don't know something. But at the exact same moment, I am actively mapping out the alternatives, questioning assumptions, devising an educated guess, and following up as quickly as I can to understand how the pieces truly fit together.

Why "Out of My Lane"?

Professional life encourages hyper-specialization: stay in your lane, defend your domain, don't stray beyond what you were hired to do. But the most interesting discoveries and the greatest creative joy happen precisely at the intersections — where music informs code, where biomechanics and Tai Chi explain ergonomic guitar posture, or where understanding signal processing demystifies both audio synthesis and large language models.

Out of My Lane is a deliberate rejection of intellectual boundaries. It's a space where I can document what I'm learning, share observations, and build small, useful things in public.

Site Philosophy: Durable & Handcrafted

The web has become bloated with layers of ephemeral frameworks, analytics scripts, and build chains that break whenever a dependency version ticks over.

This website is built differently:

  • Zero JavaScript: Everything you read here is delivered directly as pure HTML and styled with modern CSS. It loads instantaneously, respects your privacy, and has no moving parts to break.
  • Readable Typography: Scaled for effortless reading on phones, tablets, and wide desktop screens, adapting gracefully to your system's light or dark mode preference.
  • Agent Maintained: I collaborate with an LLM coding agent to maintain and refine this site, governed by strict constraints that preserve simplicity above all else.
