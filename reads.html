Reads & Bookshelf

A curated collection of books that have influenced my thinking across systems, cognitive science, craft, and human behavior. For each, I take concise notes to capture the key mental models and takeaways.

* = favorite and highly recommended read

  • Poor Charlie's Almanack *

    Peter D. Kaufman & Charlie Munger • Jan 2025

    Multidisciplinary mental models, the psychology of human misjudgment, inversion, and the immense power of compounding rational decisions over decades.

  • Unreasonable Hospitality *

    Will Guidara • Jun 2026

    Principles from Eleven Madison Park on intentionality, going beyond transactional execution, and creating unforgettable human experiences through thoughtful details.

  • The Sense of Style

    Steven Pinker • May 2024

    A linguist and cognitive scientist's perspective on modern prose: overcoming the curse of knowledge, sentence mechanics, and writing with sensory clarity.

  • How to Know a Person

    David Brooks • Mar 2024

    The practical discipline of deep seeing: listening with generous curiosity, asking Illuminator questions, and understanding people beyond their surface roles.

  • Thinking in Systems: A Primer *

    Donella H. Meadows • Sep 2023

    Feedback loops, delays, stocks, flows, and leverage points in biological, economic, and technical systems. Essential foundation for analyzing complex phenomena.
