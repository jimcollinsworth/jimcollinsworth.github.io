# Site Concept, Lanes & Content Taxonomy

> Preserved from `README.md`, `pages/blogroll.md`, and `posts/bookmarks.md`.

## Core Theme: "Out of My Lane"

The site serves as Jim Collinsworth's personal chronicle, with a focus on sharing thoughts, projects, and art related to computing and technology, alongside other interests and pursuits extending far beyond conventional lanes.

## Lanes & Topic Taxonomy

### 1. Projects
- **Data Analysis & Visualization**: Real-world data analysis, charts, and investigative metrics.
- **Site Development & Operations**: Clean web design, static architectures, and deployment workflows.
- **Home Automation**: Smart home scripting, sensors, and hardware integration.
- **No Commercials**: Commercial-free media consumption strategies and workflows.
- **Music & Art**:
  - Piano: Technique, literature, digital piano hardware enhancements.
  - Guitar: Nylon electric, study, technique.
  - Photography: Nature, sky, Chicago lakefront, botanical and street captures.
  - Drawing: Foundations, sketching, visual art.

### 2. Lanes (Interests & Focus Areas)
Each lane acts as a focused thread—containing thoughts, links, media, and ongoing investigations:
- **Finance**: Market observations, economics, personal finance.
- **Politics & Law**: Technology policy, intellectual property, industry evolution.
- **Health & Movement**:
  - Tai Chi practice.
  - Anatomy and somatic education (F.M. Alexander Technique, Said).
  - Sleep health and motor tracking.
- **Family**: Personal updates, reflections, family milestones.
- **STEM**:
  - Weather observations and atmospheric phenomena.
  - Anatomy, physiology, and biology.
- **AI & Modern Computing**: LLMs, local offline AI companions, agentic workflows.

### 3. References & Reading
- Curated books (e.g. nanotechnology, philosophy, design).
- Research papers.
- Essential URLs & regular reads (e.g. *Techdirt* for tech law, innovation history, and industry coverage).
- Key quotes and excerpts.
