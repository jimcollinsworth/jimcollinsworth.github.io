# Release v0.2 — Editorial Redesign, Bookshelf & Gallery

> Major visual redesign inspired by Calvin French-Owen and Tom MacWright, introducing dedicated reading synthesis and photo gallery streams.

---

### Overview

`v0.2` overhauled the visual presentation and content hierarchy. It introduced an editorial design system focused on long-form reading comfort, curated book notes with mental models, and a photography stream with direct links to high-resolution Google Photos albums.

---

### Key Features & Changes

- **Calvin-Style Editorial Typography**: Implemented refined typographic rhythm, comfortable body line length (60–75 characters), and automatic dark/light mode switching.
- **Dedicated Bookshelf (`reads.html`)**: Introduced structured reading synthesis capturing core takeaways and mental models.
- **MacWright-Style Photo Gallery (`gallery.html`)**: Built an image stream celebrating photography with `<figure>` and `<figcaption>` context.
- **Streamlined Navigation**: Simplified header navigation into five core destinations: `Home`, `About`, `Posts`, `Reads`, and `Gallery`.
- **Content Boundary Remediation**: Cleared placeholder text to ensure all published opinions and words originate solely from Jim's archives.
