# Release v0.7.12 — Intra-Page Provenance Attribution, Pure Markdown Shortcuts & Rule Codification

**Release Date:** September 15, 2026  
**Commit:** Milestone 49  
**Tag:** `v0.7.12`

---

### Overview

Release `v0.7.12` establishes granular intra-page authorship attribution across all site pages: lead introductions and page summaries authored by Jim are marked with the `[Mine]` provenance badge, AI-generated components are marked with the `[AI]` badge, pure Markdown shortcuts (`[Mine]`, `[AI]`, `[Me]`, `[Ours]`, `[Theirs]`, `[AI+Mine]`) are automatically compiled into accessible SVG badges by Pelican, and Section 20 is codified in site governance documents.

---

### Key Changes

1. **Intra-Page Provenance & Lead Intro Attribution (`content/pages/`, `theme/templates/`)**:
   - Added `[Mine]` provenance badges to the lead introductory paragraph and section headings across `about-this-site.md`, `apps.md`, `about.md`, `links.md`, `ai.md`, and `contact.md`.
   - Added `[Mine]` badge to `.photos-title` in `theme/templates/photos.html` and `theme/templates/ideas.html`.
   - Updated AI haiku summary heading to `[AI] Site History Haiku` in `theme/templates/about-this-site.html` and `tools/generate_site_summary.py`.

2. **Pure Markdown Provenance Shortcuts (`pelicanconf.py`)**:
   - Upgraded `_decorate_provenance_badges` in `ObsidianMarkdownReader` to parse and replace `[Mine]`, `[AI]`, `[Me]`, `[Ours]`, `[Theirs]`, `[AI+Mine]`, and `[Mine+AI]` across headings, paragraphs, and lists into accessible SVG badges with tooltips.

3. **Badge Alignment & Heading Styling (`theme/static/css/style.css`)**:
   - Added CSS rules for `.category-badge` and `.category-icon` establishing baseline alignment inside `h1`–`h3` headings and inline paragraphs.

4. **Rule Codification & Documentation (`AGENTS.md`, `.agents/agent_rules.md`, `README.md`)**:
   - Codified Section 20 ("Intra-Page Provenance & Section-Level Attribution Standards") requiring `[Mine]` badges on lead intros/summaries and `[AI]` on AI components.
   - Documented pure Markdown shortcuts and intra-page attribution in `README.md`.

5. **Test Suite Expansion (`tests/test_pelican_e2e.py`)**:
   - Added `test_intra_page_provenance_and_markdown_shortcuts` verifying that Markdown shortcuts compile to SVGs and that lead intros contain proper badges. Total tests: 59 passing.
