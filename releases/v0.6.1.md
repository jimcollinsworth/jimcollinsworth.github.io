# Release v0.6.1 — Provenance Categories (Mine, AI Generated, Ours, Theirs) & Topic Decoupling

> Replaced rigid "lanes" taxonomy with 4 mutually-exclusive Provenance Categories (`Mine`, `AI Generated`, `Ours`, `Theirs`), decoupled topics into flexible keyword tags and future custom topic pages (Tai Chi, Music, Science, Big Projects), re-pointed the header tagline directly to `/posts.html`, and replaced lane bars with lightweight stream navigation.

---

### Overview

`v0.6.1` refines the site's architectural taxonomy by eliminating artificial "lanes" category silos in favor of native, 1:1 Pelican **Provenance Categories** (`Mine`, `AI Generated`, `Ours`, `Theirs`). The site tagline *"Out of My Lane"* now links directly to the full chronological posts archive (`/posts.html`). Subject-matter organization is fully decoupled into flexible keyword tags (`tags: [...]`) and dedicated standalone topic pages (to be curated by Jim for Tai Chi, Music, Science, Big Projects, etc.).

---

### Key Changes & Upgrades

1. **Native Pelican Provenance Categories**:
   - Replaced multi-lane generator mutations with Pelican's clean, native single-category model.
   - Standardized 4 canonical provenance streams:
     - **`Mine`**: Purely original human writing, projects, and personal notes authored by Jim (default).
     - **`AI Generated`**: Material generated primarily by AI / LLMs.
     - **`Ours`**: Collaborative works created jointly (Jim + collaborators or Jim + AI).
     - **`Theirs`**: Curated external works, references, quotes, or third-party highlights.
   - Output category streams generated cleanly to `category/{slug}.html` (e.g. `category/mine.html`, `category/ours.html`).

2. **Decoupling Topics into Tags & Custom Pages**:
   - Topic exploration is freed from category constraints: posts use `tags: [music, guitar]`, `tags: [health, tai-chi]`, etc.
   - Foundation laid for future curated topic hub pages (e.g., Tai Chi, Music, Science, Big Projects) authored directly by Jim.

3. **Tagline & Stream Navigation UX**:
   - Header tagline *"Out of My Lane"* now navigates directly to `/posts.html` (All Posts).
   - Replaced `.lane-nav` and `.footer-lanes` with lightweight, pill-free `.stream-nav` (`All • Mine • Ours`) and `.footer-categories`.
   - Post metadata lines display `[TYPE] • Date • Stream` (e.g. `[PROJ] • SEP 04, 2026 • Mine`).

4. **Cleaned Repository & Asset Purge**:
   - Safely retired and deleted obsolete `lanes/` directory and `lanes.html`.

5. **Governance & Documentation**:
   - Synchronized Section 5 in `AGENTS.md` and `.agents/agent_rules.md`.
   - Updated `docs/content_authoring.md`, `README.md`, `PLANNING.md`, and `JOURNAL.md`.
   - Synchronized DevOps dashboard on `about-this-site.md` and `pyproject.toml` to `v0.6.1`.

6. **Testing & Visual Audit**:
   - Verified all 43 automated tests pass cleanly (`uv run pytest -v`).
   - Captured full-resolution responsive visual screenshots across light and dark themes.
