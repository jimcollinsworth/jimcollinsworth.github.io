# Release v0.7.7.01 — Photo Asset Size Management Policy & Zero Full-Resolution In-Repo Standards

**Release Date:** September 14, 2026  
**Commit:** Milestone 43  
**Tag:** `v0.7.7.01`

---

### Overview

Release `v0.7.7.01` establishes binding repository asset management standards for photography across governance documents, developer rules, and project tracking. Full-resolution and camera RAW files (> 500 KB) are prohibited from the Git repository and will resolve externally to Google Photos or Google Drive direct URLs. The repository hosts strictly two web-optimized size tiers: thumbnails and medium display assets.
