# Release v0.6.0 — Multi-Lane Taxonomy, Post-Type Lifecycle Evolution & Mandatory AI Attribution

> Decoupled format and lifecycle (`type`, `previous_types`) from thematic pursuit lanes (`lanes`), enabled multi-lane indexing, purged non-lanes (`Ideas`, `Projects`) from categories, designed a Zero-JS Google Sheets feedback pipeline (GitHub Issue #9), and codified Rule 14 for transparent AI author attribution.

---

### Overview

`v0.6.0` delivers a fundamental architecture upgrade to content categorization and metadata semantics. It solves the conflation of *format/lifecycle* with *subject domain* by establishing a canonical set of 5 pursuit lanes (`AI`, `Art`, `Health`, `Making`, `Music`), enabling multi-lane post membership (e.g. `[Music, Making]`), and introducing a 28-code post-type taxonomy with explicit conceptual evolution lineage (`(evolved from IDEA → WIP)`). Furthermore, this release codifies Rule 14 (Mandatory AI & LLM Author Attribution) and establishes the architectural specification for a Zero-JS feedback pipeline backed by Google Sheets and LLM summarization.

---

### Key Changes & Upgrades

1. **Pursuit Lanes Decoupled from Post Format & Lifecycle**:
   - The 5 canonical pursuit lanes are strictly: `AI`, `Art`, `Health`, `Making`, `Music`.
   - Purged `Ideas` and `Projects` from lane archives — they are now lifecycle stages (`[IDEA]`, `[PROJ]`).
   - `M.E. (Mental Entity / My Essence)` re-categorized into the `AI` pursuit lane.
   - Retired `lanes/ideas.html` and `lanes/projects.html`.

2. **Multi-Lane Membership & Dynamic Category Generation**:
   - Enhanced `ObsidianMarkdownReader` and added Pelican generator hook `assign_multi_lane_categories`.
   - Posts can now belong to multiple lanes simultaneously (e.g. Digital Piano Enhancements belongs to both `[Music, Making]`).
   - The article appears and renders in both `lanes/music.html` and `lanes/making.html`.

3. **Post-Type Taxonomy & Evolution Lineage**:
   - Introduced uppercase short codes (`[PROJ]`, `[IDEA]`, `[WIP]`, `[SPEC]`, `[VIEW]`, etc.) rendered alongside publication date and lane links across `posts.html`, `index.html`, `archives.html`, `category.html`, and `article.html`.
   - Added `previous_types` support rendering conceptual lineage (e.g., `(evolved from IDEA → WIP)`).
   - Designed 28 standard short codes across Tech, Media, Science, Life, and Arts.

4. **Zero-JS Feedback Pipeline Architecture ([GitHub Issue #9](https://github.com/jimcollinsworth/jimcollinsworth.github.io/issues/9))**:
   - Designed private Google Apps Script & Google Sheet backend to capture reader feedback without JavaScript.
   - Prevents public repository spam and defacement while preserving Jim's visibility into all raw inputs.
   - Documented LLM pre-build triage and digest flow in `docs/content_authoring.md` and `content/pages/about.md`.

5. **Governance & Agent Rules (Rule 14 Mandatory AI Attribution)**:
   - Codified Rule 14 in `.agents/agent_rules.md`: All AI-assisted, co-authored, or synthesized content must explicitly credit the specific model ID prefixed with `LLM-` (e.g., `LLM-Gemini3.8`, `LLM-Qwen3.5`).
   - Re-synchronized Rules 15 (Prohibition of Network Tunneling) and 16 (Workspace Isolation).

6. **Testing & Validation**:
   - Full test suite expanded to 43 passing tests (`uv run pytest -v`).
   - Multi-lane membership, type evolution, zero pills, and link integrity verified.
