# Release v0.6.4 — Compact Mobile Header, Streamlined Date Formats & Dense Post Listings

> Optimized mobile header layout to strictly 1 line in landscape mode and 2 lines in portrait, removed visual evolution lineage from post headers, adopted succinct Month Year date formatting (e.g. Aug 26), and implemented dense 1-2 line clamped post listings on small screens.

---

### Overview

\0.6.4\ addresses mobile viewport ergonomics based on Jim's live testing and photo submissions. On landscape phone displays, the previous multi-row header consumed fully half the screen; it has now been flattened to a single horizontal bar of under 40px. Post dates have been simplified to Month Year format, post evolution lineage has been removed from view, and mobile post listings have been condensed to compact 1-2 line clamped previews.

---

### Key Changes & Upgrades

1. **Strictly 1-Line Header on Phone Landscape**:
   - On landscape phones (\@media (orientation: landscape) and (max-height: 500px)\), the entire site header renders on a single horizontal row: \Jim Collinsworth\ | \Home Posts AI Links Photos Apps Site\ | \	heme controls\.
   - Hidden the tagline (*Out of My Lane*) on mobile and landscape phones to conserve reading space.
   - Reduced padding and margin, immediately liberating over 150px of vertical space for reading.

2. **Compact 2-Row Grid on Phone Portrait**:
   - Employs \display: contents\ to align \Jim Collinsworth\ on the left and display controls on the right (Row 1), with navigation links spanning Row 2.

3. **Streamlined Date Formatting**:
   - Dates across all article headers, post archives, category feeds, and index streams now display as \%b %y\ (e.g. \Aug 26\, \Sep 23\).

4. **Removed Evolution Lineage from Post Display**:
   - Removed \(evolved from ...)\ from visual post headers and list displays per Jim's instruction; retained in frontmatter metadata.

5. **Dense Mobile Post Listings (1-2 Lines Max)**:
   - Clamped post description text to 2 lines max with \-webkit-line-clamp: 2\ and ellipsis on mobile screens (< 640px).
   - Reduced item spacing from 2.25rem to 0.75rem with clean hairline borders, displaying 4-5 posts per mobile screen.
