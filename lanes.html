Lanes of Exploration

In traditional blogging, posts are published once and slowly buried by time. In contrast, Lanes are ongoing subject-matter tracks. Each lane is an evolving collection of notes, active experiments, reference links, and observations that are continuously edited, updated, and deepened.

1. Music & Instrument Studies

Music has been a throughline across my life — from years as a working guitarist to my current focus on adult piano studies and acoustic ergonomics.

  • Digital Piano Enhancements: Ergonomic, auditory, and structural refinements to my Kawai ES-8 digital stage piano, analyzing soundboard response and tactile feedback.
  • Cordoba Stage Nylon Guitar: Exploring chambered thin-line classical playability, tone shaping, and fusion techniques.
  • Theory & Kinesthetics: Connecting fingerboard geometry, hand biomechanics, and effortless memorization.

2. STEM & Computing

Five decades of systems architecture condensed into personal tools, local software experiments, and continuous inquiry into natural phenomena.

  • The M.E. Project (Mental Entity / My Essence): Architecture concept for an entirely offline, local wearable AI companion. Designed to observe from the user's perspective over years, learning nuances through repetition and consolidation rather than invasive bulk recording.
  • Bookmarks Data Analyzer: Developing a clean Python data pipeline to parse decades of Chrome bookmarks, generate privacy-safe taxonomies, and build a curated static internet navigator.
  • Static Web Endurance: Championing zero-JavaScript, handcrafted semantic HTML as the most resilient foundation for digital writing.
  • Lakefront Weather Observation: Documenting Chicago storm dynamics, cloud formations, and atmospheric shifts along Lake Michigan.

3. Health, Somatics & Sleep

Prioritizing non-pharmacological, evidence-based approaches to physical vitality, movement health, and restful sleep.

  • Sleep Movement & Periodic Limb Analysis: Developing an integrative plan to assess nighttime leg twitching and vocalizations. Utilizing standardized clinical self-assessments (RLSRS, PSQI, Epworth Sleepiness Scale, GAD-7) and partner observations to isolate triggers like late-night sugar and stress.
  • Tai Chi & Alexander Technique: Somatic movement study focusing on effortless skeletal alignment, mindful intention, releasing unnecessary muscular tension, and joint mobility.

4. Physical Craft & Making

Working with hands and physical materials to solve concrete problems.

  • Ulu Knife Handle Shaping: Handcrafting custom ergonomic hardwood handles for traditional Alaskan Ulu blades, matching the tool to the natural grip of the palm.
  • Workshop Tools & Restoration: Restoring hand planes, sharpening stones, and fine woodworking implements.
