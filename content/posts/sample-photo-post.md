## Image Reference & Figure Embed

In Obsidian, media can be referenced directly from `media/images/` using standard Markdown syntax:

![Chicago Lakefront Sky Composition](media/images/sky-lakefront.jpg)
*Figure 1: Deep blue water and cloud formations along Lake Michigan, Chicago.*

## Technical Details & Web Resolution

- **Local Display Tier**: Medium web-optimized preview (~250 KB) stored locally in `content/media/images/`.
- **Full Resolution Storage**: Camera originals and mega-zoom source files resolve to external Google Photos direct links.
