## Image Reference & Figure Embed

In Obsidian, media can be referenced directly from `attachments/` using standard Markdown syntax:

![Chicago Lakefront Sky Composition](attachments/sky-lakefront.jpg)

## Technical Details & Web Resolution

- **Local Display Tier**: Medium web-optimized preview (~250 KB) stored locally in `content/attachments/`.
- **Full Resolution Storage**: Camera originals and mega-zoom source files resolve to external Google Photos direct links.
